## Full description (4000 characters max)

A+ Study Lab is a free study app for the CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201) and Core 2 (220-1202) exams. I built it while making beginner A+ videos, because I wanted one place to read a topic, drill it with flashcards, then test it with real exam-style questions.

What's inside

9 modules that follow the exam domains: Mobile Devices, Networking, Hardware, Virtualization and Cloud, Hardware and Network Troubleshooting, Operating Systems, Security, Software Troubleshooting and Operational Procedures.

46 concepts, each with short lesson notes, an everyday comparison, a real example and a memory trick. Port numbers, RAID levels, Wi-Fi standards and laser printer steps all get a table you can check at a glance.

322 practice questions in five styles: multiple choice, true or false, matching, putting steps in order, and calculations like subnet hosts, RAID space and watts. Every answer explains why it's right.

215 flashcards to flip through on the bus.

How it helps you study

Get a question wrong and it comes back once before the round ends. The Weak spots quiz pulls together the questions you keep missing. Progress shows how far along you are in every concept, module and exam, so you can see what still needs work.

Make your own course

Studying for something else, like Network+ or a class at school? Settings has a Copy AI prompt button. Paste it into any AI chat with your PDF or notes, then load what it gives back. Your courses sit in a library next to the built-in A+ course, each with its own progress.

Make it yours

Light and dark mode, a glass or classic look, colour themes, and your own background photo (JPG, PNG, HEIC and more).

Private and offline

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone, and the app works with no internet.

A+ Study Lab is independent practice content written for beginners. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CompTIA. CompTIA and A+ are trademarks of CompTIA, Inc. Check the official exam objectives before you book your exam.
