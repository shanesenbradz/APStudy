Privacy Policy

A+ Study Lab · Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version

A+ Study Lab does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Who makes this app

A+ Study Lab is made by Bradley Williams, an independent developer in Jamaica. The app and its source code are published at github.com/shanesenbradz/APStudy.

What stays on your device

The app saves these things in its own storage on your phone or browser, so it can remember them next time:

  • Your quiz answers and progress
  • Courses you add yourself
  • A background photo, if you choose one
  • Your settings, such as theme and colour

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

When the app uses the internet

The app works offline. It only goes online in these cases:

  • If you pick a HEIC or TIFF photo and your phone can't open that format by itself, the app downloads a free converter script from cdn.jsdelivr.net. The photo itself is converted on your phone and never uploaded. Like any website, jsDelivr can see that a request came from your internet connection. Their policy is at jsdelivr.com.
  • If you tap a link, such as the YouTube channel, it opens in your browser or the YouTube app, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Courses you add

When you make your own course, the app gives you a prompt to paste into an AI chat of your choice. That happens outside this app, in a service you pick, under that service's privacy policy. A+ Study Lab only reads the course file you paste or open, and keeps it on your device.

Children

The app is meant for people studying for an IT certification and is not aimed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions about privacy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/shanesenbradz/APStudy/issues.
